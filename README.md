## 🌍 → 🌙 → 🔴 Energy-B(能量币 / 易币)

> 一款中大型开放世界社会模拟游戏:看一种新货币如何改变人类文明,从地球一路走向星辰大海。

### 一、这是什么游戏?

**Energy-B**(中文名:**能量币**,简称 **EB**,中文又称 **易币**)是一款中大型开放性社会模拟游戏。

你将亲历一种全新的货币——能量币——从诞生、发起,到逐步取代纸币的全过程;并见证它如何悄悄改变人们生活的方方面面。随着人类走出地球,迁往月球、火星,再探索更遥远的新星球,**能量币始终是贯穿整个游戏的唯一主线**:人类用它交换生产资料、建立社群、开拓家园。

### 二、核心设定

- **一条主线**:能量币(EB)串起整个游戏——个人生活、人与人的交往、社群与社会的运转、星球环境的改变,全部围绕它展开。
- **货币演进**:从最初的发起与试点,到被社会接纳,再到完全取代纸币,每个阶段都带来社会细节的变化(消费、工作、交易、信任、制度……)。
- **星际扩展**:地球 → 月球 → 火星 → 更多新星球。无论走到哪里,人类使用的始终是能量币。
- **资源交换**:探索宇宙的过程中,人类以 EB 交换生产资料,推动殖民地的建立与发展。
- **逐关推进**:一步步"闯关"式的阶段目标,让玩家在漫长的文明进程中始终保持乐趣与成就感。
- **有趣第一**:再宏大的设定,也必须"好玩"。

## 🌍 → 🌙 → 🔴 Energy-B

> A large-scale open-world social simulation game about how one new currency reshapes human civilization, from Earth to the stars.

### 1. What is Energy-B?

**Energy-B** (Chinese: **能量币**, abbreviated **EB**, also called **易币 "Yi Coin"**) is a mid-to-large-scale open-ended social simulation game.

You will experience the entire journey of a brand-new currency, from its creation and launch to its gradual replacement of paper money, and watch how it quietly changes every detail of daily life. As humanity leaves Earth for the Moon, Mars, and then farther worlds, **Energy-B remains the single thread running through the entire game**: people use it to trade for production resources, build communities, and found new homes.

### 2. Core Design

- **One main thread**: EB ties everything together, including individual lives, human relationships, communities and society, and the transformation of the planets themselves.
- **Currency evolution**: From the initial launch and pilot, to social acceptance, to fully replacing paper money. Each stage changes society in concrete ways (consumption, work, trade, trust, institutions…).
- **Interplanetary expansion**: Earth → Moon → Mars → new worlds. Wherever humans go, they use Energy-B.
- **Resource exchange**: While exploring the universe, humanity exchanges EB for production resources to build and grow colonies.
- **Stage-by-stage progression**: Level-style goals keep the long civilizational journey engaging and rewarding.
- **Fun first**: No matter how grand the concept, the game must be enjoyable to play.

### 3. Features

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Social simulation | Work, consumption, relationships, communities, institutions |
| Currency evolution | Watch a currency change the world |
| Planetary change | Planets transform as civilization develops |
| Open gameplay | Mid-to-large open world with high freedom |
| Sci-fi foresight | A simulation of a future where energy coins replace paper money |
